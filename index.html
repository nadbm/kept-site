Other things it does

Search inside scans

Search for a policy number or a name and Kept finds it in the text of the scan, not only in file names.

Renewals replace the old copy

When you file a new licence, the old one moves to the archive and the reminder moves to the new expiry date.

Missing documents are flagged

If an application needs a recent pay stub and your newest is from August, Kept says so.

Ordinary files in Finder

Documents are saved as clearly named PDFs in a folder you choose. If you stop using Kept, the files are still there.

Your passport is not going to anyone's cloud.

These are some of the most sensitive files you own. Kept reads them with the text recognition built into macOS, on your own machine, and has no server to send them to.

You can check this with any network monitor. Kept makes no connections.

  • Read on your MacText recognition runs on-device. No AI service sees your documents.
  • No accountThere is no sign-up, so Kept holds no information about you.
  • No internet permissionKept ships without network access, and macOS enforces that. Purchases and updates go through the App Store, and calendar sync is done by macOS.
  • Protected by your MacYour documents sit on your own disk, protected by your Mac login, and by FileVault if you use it. Touch ID can lock the app.

Why this exists

I look after the paperwork for my family: our IDs, a couple of properties and their leases, the car, the insurance and the tax slips. Nearly all of it expires or renews, and I had no reliable way to be reminded.

When a mortgage application asked for most of it at once, I went looking for an app that would file documents as I dropped them in, put the renewals on my calendar and not make me fill in forms. I couldn't find one that kept everything on my own Mac, so I built Kept.

Questions

What if Kept reads a date wrong?

Nothing is filed until you confirm it. Kept highlights each value on the page where it found it, so a wrong date is easy to spot, and you can fix a single field before pressing Return. Passports get a second check against the machine-readable lines at the bottom.

If it has no internet access, how do I pay and get updates?

Through the Mac App Store. The store handles payment and updates, so Kept itself never needs to go online. Calendar events sync the same way: Kept hands them to the Mac's Calendar, and macOS syncs them to iCloud, Google or Exchange.

Does it work with documents from my country?

Version 1 is tuned for Canadian documents, and US formats are next. Passports from any country work, because they share one international layout. Anything Kept doesn't recognise is still filed and searchable, and you can set its date once by hand.

Can I add tabs for other things, like a business or a pet?

Yes. Add a tab, name it, and pick an icon and a colour. Documents you file under it get their own reminders like everything else.

What if my Documents folder syncs to iCloud?

Then Apple syncs it, as it does your other files. Kept itself sends nothing. If you want everything fully local, pick a folder outside iCloud Drive.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Reminders reach your phone through your calendar, and you can scan with your iPhone's camera straight into Kept on the Mac.

What happens when version 2 comes out?

Your copy keeps working, with every version 1 update. A future version 2 would be an optional paid upgrade.
